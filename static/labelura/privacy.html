The short version

Your avoid list, your photos and your scans never leave your phone. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. The only things that leave the phone are a barcode you choose to look up, a report you choose to send, the receipt Apple needs to confirm a Pro purchase, and anonymous counts of how many people reach each onboarding step.

Effective 13 October 2026. This policy covers the Labelura app for iPhone, made by Mihai Serban, who is responsible for the data described here.

What Labelura does

Labelura reads the ingredient list on a food label, or looks up a barcode, and shows anything from your own avoid list, the allergens, the additives, a processing estimate and the nutrition, with the source for each flag.

What stays on your phone

  • Your avoid list. The allergens, diets and ingredients you choose, and anything you type yourself, are kept only on your phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Photos and label text. Text recognition runs on your phone, using Apple's Vision framework. Photos you take or pick are never uploaded.
  • Scan history. Each result, with its label text, product name and photo if you took one, is kept on your phone so you can reopen it.
  • Free result counter. The app records on the phone when you used each free full result, to apply the limit of 5 in any 7 days. It is kept when you delete your scans and removed when you delete the app.

You can erase your scans in the app under Settings, Delete all scans, and clear your list on the My list screen. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. If you use iCloud Backup, Apple may include the app's data in your own encrypted device backup, as it does for every app. That backup is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

What leaves your phone

  • Barcode lookups. When you scan a barcode, the app asks Open Food Facts for that product. The request carries only the barcode number and the app's name. Your list never goes with it, and the app checks the answer against your list on the phone. Open Food Facts is run by a non-profit; see its privacy policy.
  • Onboarding step counts. The first time you reach a step, such as the welcome screen, the list screen, the plans screen or your first result, the app asks our server to add 1 to that step's count for the day. Nothing else is sent: no device identifier, no list items, no label text. We use the totals only to see where people give up.
  • Reports you choose to send. If you tap "Report a wrong result" and send it, we receive the label text, the note you wrote, the rules version and whether the scan came from a photo, a barcode or pasted text, a random number the app made for that one scan, and the time. Reports carry no name, email, device identifier or avoid list, so they are not linked to you. Do not write anything about your health in the note. We use reports only to correct the rules and keep them only as long as that takes.
  • Purchases. Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Our purchases provider, RevenueCat, receives your App Store receipt and an anonymous ID it creates, to confirm your plan. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Who handles it for us

  • Convex hosts our server and stores reports and daily step counts, in the European Union (Ireland). Convex privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat confirms purchases, as described above.
  • Open Food Facts answers barcode lookups, as described above.
  • Apple processes payments and subscriptions.

Like any web server, these services see your internet address while delivering a request. We do not store it with reports or use it to identify you.

Health and belief information

An avoid list can reveal an allergy, a health condition or a religious belief, such as choosing halal or kosher. That is why it never leaves your phone, is not on our server, and is never used for advertising or shared with anyone.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking. The app does not ask to track you across other apps.
  • No third-party analytics service and no crash reporting service.
  • No account, no email address and no sign-in.
  • We never sell data or share it for marketing.

Apple's App Privacy label

On the App Store, Labelura lists Data Not Linked to You: Purchases, User Content and Usage Data. That matches this policy: the purchase receipt, the text of reports you choose to send, and the anonymous onboarding step counts.

Children

Labelura is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you can ask to see, correct or delete data about you. Because reports carry nothing that identifies you, we cannot find yours by your identity. If you remember what a report said, write to us and we will find and delete it. Everything else is on your phone and under your control. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what the app does with data, this page will say what and why before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

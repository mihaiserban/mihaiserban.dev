What BumpLabel is

BumpLabel reads the ingredient list on a skincare or cosmetic label and shows which ingredients carry a pregnancy or breastfeeding concern. Every flag names the ingredient, the concern and the source, so you can read it yourself or show it to your midwife or doctor. It is information, not medical advice.

Common questions

The app could not read my label.
Lay the product flat or hold it still in good light, and fill the frame with the ingredient list. For lists that wrap around a bottle, turn on "Several photos of one label". You can also tap Paste and paste the list from the brand's website.
Why do pregnancy and breastfeeding give different answers?
Because the evidence is different. An ingredient that is a concern during pregnancy can be fine while breastfeeding, and the other way round. Change your mode in Settings or on any result.
What does "no ingredients of concern found" mean?
That none of the ingredients we recognised are flagged by our rules. It is the most the app ever says, and it is not a promise that a product is right for you. Ingredients we could not recognise are listed separately.
How many free scans do I get?
5 label scans every 7 days. Each one comes back 7 days after you use it. Looking up a single ingredient is always free and unlimited.
How do I cancel Pro?
Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then BumpLabel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel it. The full details are in the terms of use.
I bought Pro but the app shows Free.
In the app, open Settings and tap "Restore purchases", signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with.
How do I get a refund?
Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
What happens to my photos and information?
Photos are read on your phone and never uploaded. Your mode and due date stay on your phone too. The full privacy policy explains the rest.
How do I delete my data?
In the app, open Settings, then Your data, then Delete my data. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored.
How are results decided?
In the app, open Settings, then "Methodology and sources". It explains the rules, the result bands and every source cited.

What Labelura is

Labelura reads the ingredient list on a food label, or looks up a barcode, and shows what is on it: anything from your own avoid list, the allergens, the additives, how processed it looks and, if you add a photo of the table, the nutrition. Every flag names the source it comes from. It is information, not medical advice.

Common questions

What can I put on my list?
The 14 EU allergens and their members (such as hazelnut or wheat), diets such as vegan, vegetarian, halal or kosher, ingredients such as palm oil, and anything you type yourself. Diet presets check the ingredients only. They are not a certification.
What does "Not found on this label" mean?
That none of your list items were read in the ingredients we recognised. It is never a promise that a product is free of them. Recipes change, and a label can be misread, so always check the pack. When we could only read part of a label, the app says so and does not show "Not found".
Does "may contain" count?
Yes, by default. A "may contain" warning shows on your list result as its own line. You can turn off "Count “may contain” as found" on the My list screen.
The app could not read my label.
Hold the pack still in good light and fill the frame with the ingredient list. For lists that wrap around a pack, take several photos of one label. You can also scan the barcode, or tap Paste and paste the list from the shop's website.
Why does a barcode result ask me to photograph the label?
Barcode results come from Open Food Facts, a database people edit. It can be out of date for the pack in your hand, so a photo of the label is the check that counts.
What is free?
Scanning and the check against your list are free on every scan, with no limit. You also get 5 full results in any 7 days, with additive levels, the processing estimate and nutrition. Each one comes back 7 days after you use it. Free history shows your last 30 days.
How do I cancel Pro?
Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Labelura. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel it. The full details are in the terms of use.
I bought Pro but the app shows Free.
In the app, open Settings and tap "Restore purchase", signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with.
How do I get a refund?
Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
What happens to my list and my photos?
Your list, your photos and your scans stay on your phone. Photos are read on the phone and never uploaded. The full privacy policy explains the rest.
How do I delete my data?
In the app, open Settings, then "Delete all scans". Clear your list on the My list screen. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.
How are results decided?
In the app, open Settings, then "Methodology and sources". It explains how allergens, additives, processing and nutrition are read, and lists every source.

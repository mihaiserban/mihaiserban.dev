The short version

Your photos, your pregnancy or breastfeeding mode and your due date never leave your phone. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. The only things we ever receive are a report you choose to send, the receipt Apple needs to confirm a Pro purchase, and anonymous counts of how many people reach each onboarding step.

Effective 11 October 2026. This policy covers the BumpLabel app for iPhone, made by Mihai Serban, who is responsible for the data described here.

What BumpLabel does

BumpLabel reads the ingredient list on a skincare or cosmetic label and shows which ingredients carry a pregnancy or breastfeeding concern, with the source for each one.

What stays on your phone

  • Photos and label text. Text recognition runs on your phone, using Apple's Vision framework. Photos you take or pick are never uploaded.
  • Your mode and due date. Whether you chose pregnancy, breastfeeding, trying to conceive or just checking, and the optional weeks along (stored as a due date), are kept only on your phone. They are never sent to us.
  • Scan history. Each result, with its label text and photo if you took one, is kept on your phone so you can reopen it.
  • Free scan counter. The app counts your scans in the last 7 days on the phone to apply the free limit.

You can erase all of this in the app under Settings, Your data, Delete my data, or by deleting the app. If you use iCloud Backup, Apple may include the app's data in your own encrypted device backup, as it does for every app. That backup is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

What leaves your phone

  • Rule updates. When the app opens, it downloads the latest ingredient rule table from our server. The request carries no personal data.
  • Onboarding step counts. The first time you reach a step, such as the welcome screen, the plans screen or your first result, the app asks our server to add 1 to that step's count for the day. Nothing else is sent: no device identifier, no mode or due date, no label text. We use the totals only to see where people give up.
  • Reports you choose to send. If you tap "Report a wrong result" and send it, we receive the label text, the note you wrote, a random number the app made for that one scan, and the time. Reports carry no name, email, device identifier or health information, so they are not linked to you. We use them only to correct the rule table and keep them only as long as that takes.
  • Purchases. Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Our purchases provider, RevenueCat, receives your App Store receipt and an anonymous ID it creates, to confirm your plan. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Who handles it for us

  • Convex hosts our server and stores reports and daily step counts, in the European Union (Ireland). Convex privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat confirms purchases, as described above.
  • Apple processes payments and subscriptions.

Like any web server, these services see your internet address while delivering a request. We do not store it with reports or use it to identify you.

Health information

Your mode and due date are health information. They never leave your phone, are not on our server, and are never used for advertising or shared with anyone.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking. The app does not ask to track you across other apps.
  • No third-party analytics service and no crash reporting service.
  • No account, no email address and no sign-in.
  • We never sell data or share it for marketing.

Apple's App Privacy label

On the App Store, BumpLabel lists Data Not Linked to You: Purchases, User Content and Usage Data. That matches this policy: the purchase receipt, the text of reports you choose to send, and the anonymous onboarding step counts.

Children

BumpLabel is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you can ask to see, correct or delete data about you. Because reports carry nothing that identifies you, we cannot find yours by your identity. If you remember what a report said, write to us and we will find and delete it. Everything else is on your phone and under your control. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what the app does with data, this page will say what and why before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
